Privacy Policy

Bitsocial Forge Inc. is responsible for the personal data described here. Questions and requests: privacy@bitsocialforge.com.

What we collect

  • Your files and facts about them: content hash, size, type, upload time, and the filename, which becomes part of the link unless you leave it out.
  • Your IP address, linked to the files it uploaded. We delete it after about 90 days. If a file is reported or removed, the related records are kept for up to one year from upload, then deleted.
  • Rate-limit counters keyed to your IP address, deleted after two days.
  • Reports you send through the report form: the link, reason, details, and your IP address, kept as records of how we handled the report.
  • Emails you send us, kept as long as needed to deal with them and as legal records.
  • Short-lived server logs used to run and secure the service.

We remove EXIF (including GPS location), XMP, IPTC, and comments from JPEG, PNG, and WebP files before storing them, keeping only the rotation setting so photos display the right way up. We remove container metadata, including location, from MP4 and WebM videos. GIF and SWF files are stored exactly as uploaded, and an image that cannot be processed safely is stored as uploaded. Remove sensitive information yourself before uploading.

What we do not do

No accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. This site sets no cookies of its own. We do not sell or share personal data for advertising. We do not track who views files; Cloudflare processes request data, such as IP addresses, to deliver them.

Who processes data for us

  • Cloudflare delivers and protects the service, stores files, runs the browser check before uploads (Turnstile privacy addendum), and scans served files for known child sexual abuse material. Cloudflare privacy policy.
  • OpenAI receives new images, and still frames sampled from new videos, for automated moderation through its API. OpenAI does not train on API data by default and may keep it for up to 30 days to detect abuse. OpenAI API privacy.
  • Our server host in the United States runs the upload service.

When we disclose data

We disclose data when the law requires it, including reports of apparent child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), and when needed to protect someone from serious harm.
